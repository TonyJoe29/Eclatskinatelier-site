# EclatSkinAtelier Summer Journal Affiliate Design

Date: June 6, 2026

## Objective

Turn EclatSkinAtelier into an audience-first English beauty journal that earns trust through useful seasonal stories and routines, then routes qualified readers toward verified Amazon affiliate products.

The immediate editorial window is June 7-20, 2026. The primary theme is Summer Beauty.

## Decision

Use a hybrid structure:

1. Summer Journal as the main homepage experience.
2. Shop by Category as secondary navigation.
3. Product recommendations embedded inside guides and routines.
4. Full searchable product browser retained as a utility near the bottom of the page.

This keeps the editorial rhythm inspired by By Mable Grace without copying its identity, content, or exact layouts.

## Audience And Conversion Model

The homepage prioritizes discovery and trust rather than immediate selling.

Reader path:

1. Enter through a summer story, category, Pin, or Reel.
2. Learn which product format fits a specific need.
3. Compare two or three relevant options.
4. Open a routine or product note.
5. Use a clearly disclosed affiliate link when ready.

Primary success signals:

- Article and category engagement.
- Product-card and routine-detail clicks.
- Pinterest outbound clicks.
- Saves and repeat visits.
- Verified Amazon affiliate clicks.
- Qualifying orders.

## Interaction Requirements

- Every homepage gateway applies a real category filter.
- Browser search and filters work together.
- Active filter can be represented in the URL.
- Product links open in a new tab with `nofollow sponsored noopener`.
- Product images have descriptive alt text.
- Keyboard focus remains visible.
- Mobile navigation remains usable without hiding categories.
- Reduced-motion preference disables nonessential reveals.
- Watchlist products never show an Amazon purchase button.

## Measurement And Iteration

Review every 72 hours.

Scale a product when it earns:

- An outbound click.
- Two or more saves.
- Top-three account placement.
- A same-day increase in affiliate clicks.

Pause a creative angle after five fresh assets produce no saves, no outbound clicks, and weak distribution. Do not remove the product from the bank solely because one Pin underperforms.

The database should eventually support product-level funnel reporting:

`content published -> impressions -> saves -> outbound clicks -> affiliate clicks -> orders`

## Error And Integrity Handling

- Broken official images fall back to the existing local product art.
- Missing affiliate URLs create a review-queue item.
- Invalid category parameters fall back to all products.
- Empty categories show a helpful message and related stories.
- No health outcome or product-performance claim may exceed the cited source or user-provided experience.
- Third-party creator material must retain credit and cannot be presented as EclatSkinAtelier testing.
