# Site Roadmap

## Phase 1: Private Workspace

- Keep repository private.
- Use homepage as hub for Instagram, Pinterest and Linktree traffic.
- Add product art placeholders now, replace with own photos or Amazon-compliant SiteStripe/PA-API images later.
- Add 1-2 posts per week.

## Phase 2: Blog Structure

Core categories:

- Skincare Routines
- K-Beauty
- Acne Care
- SPF + Summer Skin
- Makeup Under $20
- Bodycare
- Hair + Scalp
- Product Reviews

Core post formats:

- "Best [category] on Amazon"
- "Simple [skin concern] routine"
- "What I would buy first"
- "Trending product research"
- "Review after trying"
- "Shop the Reel"

## Phase 3: Public Launch

Before making the site public:

- Replace placeholder art where exact product imagery matters.
- Confirm every affiliate link uses the correct tracking ID.
- Add privacy/disclosure pages.
- Connect the public URL to Linktree, Instagram and Pinterest.
- Create Pinterest boards that match site categories.

## Weekly Site Update Rhythm

- Monday: add one product card.
- Wednesday: add one short post draft.
- Friday: update "Current Picks" with the best-performing product.
- Sunday: review clicks, saves and comments; plan next week.
