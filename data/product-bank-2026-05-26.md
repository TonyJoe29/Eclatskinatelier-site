## Research Notes

Amazon Associates:

- Amazon says applicants have 180 days to refer a sale through Associates links.
- Once 3 qualifying sales are referred, Amazon evaluates the application.
- Amazon qualifying purchase rules depend on properly formatted Special Links, a customer click, a session window, and the order being paid/shipped/streamed/downloaded as required by the policy.
- Practical implication: do not optimize only for views. Optimize for qualified clicks from people ready to buy.

Pinterest:

- Pinterest positions the platform as shopping-native: the No. 1 reason people use Pinterest is to discover new brands and products.
- Pinterest recommends fresh original Pins, high-quality images/video, 2:3 aspect ratio, 15-60 second videos, consistent publishing, avoiding duplicate Pins, using Pinterest Trends, and reviewing conversion-driving Pins.
- Pinterest says Pins can gain engagement hours, days, months or even years after posting. Do not judge a Pin only in the first 24 hours.
- Pinterest reported 619M monthly active users for Q4 2025 and more than 80B monthly searches, so the platform should be treated as a visual search engine, not just a feed.
- Pinterest Predicts 2026 is useful because it is based on keyword searches and visual searches. Pinterest says 88% of its trend predictions came true over the past six years, and that Predicts-related checkouts increased 68% YoY in the cited period.

Beauty / Product Demand:

- BeautyMatter's Amazon Q1 2026 Top 25 highlighted routine-driven, repeat-purchase products: Medicube Zero Pore Pad 2.0, eos Vanilla Cashmere Lotion, Hero Mighty Patch, Neutrogena Makeup Remover Wipes, Maybelline Sky High Mascara, Biodance mask, Amazon Basics cotton rounds, The Ordinary Glycolic Acid, Clean Skin Club towels and Nizoral shampoo in the Top 10.
- The same report noted skincare dominated the Top 25 and value-priced proven products gained share. This supports under-$20 products and simple routine staples.
- Allure's 2026 Amazon K-beauty list supports COSRX Snail Mucin, Anua PDRN moisturizer, Round Lab Birch sunscreen and Mixsoon PDRN cleanser as high-interest K-beauty content lanes.
- Pinterest Predicts beauty-adjacent trends that fit the brand: Scent Stacking, Glitchy Glam, Gimme Gummy, Cool Blue and Extra Celestial. These should influence visuals and titles, not replace conversion products.

## Strategy Decision

Use concentration with creative variance:

- 70% of assets: conversion core products.
- 20% of assets: trend tests.
- 10% of assets: routine/bundle content that pushes people to the site or Linktree.

Why this is better than pure variety:

- Amazon needs 3 sales, not a broad brand awareness study.
- Pinterest rewards fresh creative, not duplicate uploads. Fresh creative can still point to the same product.
- Your Linktree click rate is high, so the biggest improvement is sending more qualified people to a smaller set of clear product choices.

## Asset Rules For Every Product

Pinterest Pin formulas:

1. Search Pin: keyword-forward title, clean product/routine image.
2. Save Pin: checklist, mini routine, "3 ways to use", "what to pair with".
3. Click Pin: stronger CTA, product close-up, "shop the exact routine".
4. Comparison Pin: product A vs product B, "which one fits your routine?"
5. Trend Pin: scent stacking, glass skin, SPF under makeup, gummy/gloss, cool blue, soft glam.

Instagram Reel formulas:

1. Product-in-hand hook in first 2 seconds.
2. Texture / application close-up.
3. One clear sentence on screen.
4. CTA: "I put the Amazon link in the current picks."
5. If untested: say "researched Amazon find" or "product I would test for this routine", not "review".

## Decision Rules

Scale a product if any of these happen within 72 hours:

- 1+ outbound click.
- 2+ saves.
- 25+ impressions from a fresh account.
- Top 3 placement in Pinterest analytics.
- Linktree clicks rise on the same posting day.
- Amazon clicks rise the same day or next day.

Pause a product if:

- 5 fresh assets over 7 days produce 0 saves, 0 outbound clicks and weak impressions.
- It gets views but no clicks while another product gets clicks.
- The visual is too hard to make clear in 1 second.

Do not pause too early:

- Pinterest content can resurface later.
- Judge first by directional signals, not by same-day sales.
