Simple Skincare Routine For Beginners

A beginner routine should feel boring in the best way: cleanse, moisturize, protect in the morning, and add only one extra product when you know why it belongs there. If your skin is irritated, dry or breaking out, the answer is not always more products.

AM routine

Step 1

Gentle cleanse or rinse

If your skin is dry or sensitive, you may not need a full cleanse every morning. Keep the step gentle.

Step 2

Light moisturizer

Choose a moisturizer that keeps your skin comfortable without making SPF or makeup pill.

Step 3

SPF

Use sunscreen as the morning non-negotiable. For makeup content, focus on texture, tint and how it layers.

PM routine

Step 1

Remove makeup or SPF

A cleansing balm can make sense if you wear makeup or heavier SPF. Follow with a gentle cleanse if needed.

Step 2

Moisturize

Barrier-friendly moisturizer is often more useful than adding another active too soon.

Optional

One treatment or mask

Pick one: pimple patch, hydrating mask or exfoliant. Do not add all three in the same week if your skin is already reactive.

What to avoid

Do not build a beginner routine around too many exfoliants, retinoids or trend products at once. If skin feels hot, tight, peeling or painful, pause the extras and consider asking a professional.
